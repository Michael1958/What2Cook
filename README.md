# What2Cook 🍲

**Home from work with no idea what to cook?** Type the ingredients you already have, and What2Cook suggests Greek recipes you can make right now, with clear steps, cooking times, heat levels and nutrition per serving.

Free, in **Greek and English**, and nothing to install. It runs in any phone, tablet or computer browser.

## Key features

- 🇬🇷 **Greek recipes first:** 69 recipes, 67 of them traditional Greek home dishes
- ⌨️ **Type ingredients your own way:** in Greek, English or Greeklish, separated by commas
- 🧺 **Tap to add:** 66 ingredients in 8 categories, and tapping a tile adds it to the text box
- ⭐ **Always several suggestions:** a Best choice plus at least 3 more recipes
- ⏱ **Clear steps:** every step shows its time and heat level (Low, Medium, High or Oven °C)
- ⚖️ **Exact quantities:** only grams, ml and pieces, never spoons or pinches
- 📊 **Nutrition per serving:** energy, protein, carbohydrates, fat, fibre and salt
- ⚠️ **Allergens and diets:** Vegetarian, Vegan and Lenten (Νηστίσιμο) filters, plus 7 allergens you can exclude
- ❤️ **Saved recipes** and a 🛒 **shopping list**, stored only on your device
- 🔊 **Read aloud:** hear the ingredients and steps while you cook
- 📱 **No installation, no account, no ads**

## How to use

### 1. Enter what you have

Open the app. The **Ingredients** screen appears first.

You can add ingredients in two ways:

- **Type them** in the box at the top, separated by commas.
  Example: `ντομάτα, αυγά, feta, kotopoulo`
- **Tap the ingredient tiles** below the box. Each tile you tap is added to the box automatically. Tap it again to remove it.

Tips:
- Greek, English and Greeklish all work, for example `ντομάτες`, `tomatoes` or `domates`.
- Plurals, missing accents and small typos are understood.
- You can also separate ingredients with "και" or "and".
- Pressing **Enter** adds a comma for the next ingredient.
- While you type, the app suggests matching ingredients. Tap a suggestion to add it.
- Under the box you see what the app understood:
  - ✓ **Ντομάτα ← domates** means it was recognised
  - ✕ **pizza, not recognised** means it is not in the ingredient list

### 2. Find recipes

Tap **Find recipes** (Βρες συνταγές). The results update as you change your ingredients.

### 3. Choose a recipe

The top card is the **Best choice**, with a short reason, for example "You have all the main ingredients · Ready in 17 minutes". Tap 🔄 to see another idea.

Below it, recipes are grouped as:

| Group | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🍃 **Cook now** | You have all the main ingredients |
| 🛒 **Almost ready** | Only 1 or 2 ingredients are missing, and they are listed |
| 🧩 **With some of your ingredients** | Uses part of what you have |
| 💡 **More ideas** | Quick recipes, shown when few recipes match |
| ⚠ **Outside your settings** | Shown only when your time or oven settings leave too few recipes. Each card explains why, for example "needs an oven" |

### 4. Cook

Open a recipe to see:

- **Ingredients:** ✓ you have it, ✕ missing, ⇄ a possible swap (for example vinegar instead of lemon), · basic item such as salt or olive oil
- **Steps:** time and heat level for each step. Tap a step to mark it done.
- **Nutrition:** values per serving and allergens

Use **−** and **+** to change the number of servings (1 to 20). All quantities update, including the amounts written inside the steps.

### 5. Save and shop

- Tap the ❤️ heart to save a recipe.
- Tap **Add missing items to the list** to send missing ingredients to your shopping list.
- Tap **I cooked this** so the same recipe is not suggested first again for 7 days.

## All functionality

### Ingredients screen
- Comma-separated text box in Greek, English or Greeklish
- Live suggestions while typing
- Recognised and not-recognised ingredients shown under the box
- 66 ingredients in 8 categories: Vegetables, Meat, Fish & seafood, Dairy & eggs, Pasta, rice & bread, Legumes, Herbs & spices, Pantry basics
- Tiles stay in sync with the text box
- **Clear all** button
- Ingredient counter and **Find recipes** button

### Recipes screen
- Ingredient box available here too, so you can change ingredients without going back
- Summary bar showing your active settings, with a **Change** button
- Best choice with a reason and a 🔄 button for another idea
- Match percentage on every recipe
- Missing ingredients listed on each card
- Cooking time, calories per serving and diet tags on each card
- At least 4 recipes shown whenever your diet and allergen settings allow it

### Recipe screen
- Total time, difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard) and servings
- Servings from 1 to 20, with all quantities recalculated
- Ingredient list with ✓, ✕, ⇄ swap and · basic markers
- Optional ingredients marked as such, with a note if one does not fit your diet
- Step-by-step instructions with:
  - time in minutes
  - heat: 🔪 No heat, 🔥 Low, 🔥🔥 Medium, 🔥🔥🔥 High, ♨ Oven with °C
  - "at the same time" for steps done in parallel
  - tap to mark a step as done
- Soaking reminder for dried beans and chickpeas (soaking time is not included in the total time)
- Nutrition per serving: energy (kcal), protein, carbohydrates, fat, fibre and salt
- Allergens, including allergens found only in optional ingredients
- Tags: Vegan, Vegetarian, Lenten, Traditional Greek recipe
- 🔊 Read aloud in Greek or English
- ❤️ Save
- 🛒 Add missing items to the shopping list
- ✅ I cooked this

### Saved screen
- All saved recipes in one list
- Tap to open, ✕ to remove

### What to buy (shopping list)
- Add your own items by typing
- Items added from recipes show the quantity and the recipe they are for
- Tick items as bought
- **Delete bought items** or **Clear all**
- **Send list** through your phone's share menu (Viber, WhatsApp, email and others), or copy it on a computer
- The bottom menu shows how many items are still to buy

### Settings
- **Language:** Ελληνικά or English (also the ΕΛ | EN button at the top)
- **Diet:** All, Vegetarian, Vegan, Lenten (Νηστίσιμο)
- **Exclude allergens:** Gluten, Milk, Eggs, Fish, Shellfish, Molluscs, Celery
- **Maximum time:** No limit, 20, 30, 45 or 60 minutes
- **Equipment:** I have an oven (on or off)
- **About:** app name, version and creator
- **Delete all data** on this device

## How recipes are matched

- Each recipe ingredient has a role: **main**, **important**, **optional** or **basic**.
- The **match %** shows how many of the main and important ingredients you have.
- **Basic items** (olive oil, salt, pepper, water) never lower the match. No ingredient is assumed, but basics are listed so you can add them to the shopping list.
- **Optional items** never lower the match.
- If an ingredient is missing but you have a suitable swap, it counts as partly available and the swap is shown.
- Ranking also considers how many of your ingredients the recipe uses, whether it is a Greek recipe, your time limit, and whether you cooked it in the last 7 days.
- **Diet and allergen settings are never relaxed.** A recipe that breaks them is never shown.
- Time and oven settings may be relaxed **only** to make sure you always see several recipes. Such recipes appear in a separate "Outside your settings" section with the reason clearly marked.

## Privacy and data

- No account, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.
- The app makes no calls to any server or external service.
- Your ingredients, saved recipes, shopping list and settings are stored **only in your browser on your device**.
- Clearing your browser data, or using **Settings → Delete all data**, removes them.

## Install on GitHub Pages

The whole app is a single file: `index.html`. There is no build step and nothing to install.

1. Sign in to GitHub and create a new **public** repository, for example `What2Cook`.
2. Select **Add file → Upload files**.
3. Upload `index.html` and this `README.md`.
4. Select **Commit changes**.
5. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
6. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to **Deploy from a branch**, choose the **main** branch and the **/(root)** folder, then select **Save**.
7. Wait 1 to 2 minutes. Your app will be available at:
   `https://<your-github-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`

To use it like an app on a phone, open the link in the browser and choose **Add to Home screen**.

## Updating the app

1. Upload the new `index.html` to the repository and replace the old one.
2. Wait 1 to 2 minutes for GitHub Pages to update.
3. On your phone, refresh the page. If you still see the old version, clear the site data for the page in your browser settings, then open it again.
4. Check the version number at the bottom of the screen ("Created by: Michael Gerontides · v1.5").

Saved recipes and the shopping list are kept after an update, unless you clear the site data.

## Limitations

- **Nutrition values are estimates** calculated from typical values for each ingredient. They are not professional dietary advice.
- **Only ingredients in the app's list are recognised.** Other words are marked "not recognised", never guessed.
- **Large dishes** such as moussaka or pastitsio need many ingredients, so they usually appear under "With some of your ingredients" with the missing items listed.
- **Read aloud** depends on the voices installed on your device. Greek requires a Greek voice.
- Recipes use food icons, not photos.
- An internet connection is needed to open the page.

## Στα ελληνικά

**What2Cook** σε βοηθά να αποφασίσεις τι θα μαγειρέψεις με ό,τι έχεις ήδη στο σπίτι.

**Πώς λειτουργεί:**
1. Γράψε τα υλικά σου με κόμμα, στα ελληνικά, αγγλικά ή greeklish (π.χ. `ντομάτα, αυγά, feta, kotopoulo`), ή πάτησε τα εικονίδια των υλικών.
2. Πάτησε **Βρες συνταγές**.
3. Διάλεξε συνταγή. Θα δεις ποσότητες σε γραμμάρια, ml ή τεμάχια, χρόνο και ένταση φωτιάς για κάθε βήμα, και διατροφική αξία ανά μερίδα.
4. Αποθήκευσε τις αγαπημένες σου συνταγές και φτιάξε λίστα για ψώνια.

69 συνταγές (67 ελληνικές), φίλτρα για νηστίσιμα, χορτοφαγικά και αλλεργιογόνα, ανάγνωση συνταγής φωναχτά. Δωρεάν, χωρίς εγκατάσταση και χωρίς λογαριασμό. Όλα τα δεδομένα μένουν μόνο στη συσκευή σου.
